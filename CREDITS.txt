Chess Quest: The Lost Crown
Game code, content, original board geometry and guidebook vector artwork: created for this project, 2026. MIT license (see LICENSE).
No third-party character or concept artwork is included. The previously referenced second concept image was not supplied with this request.

Standard 8x8 browser rules: chess.js 1.4.0, Jeff Hlywa and contributors, BSD-2-Clause.
https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js
License and exact source: vendor/chess.js.LICENSE and package-lock.json (npm ci).
Native GDScript rules mirror is independently tested against chess.js fixtures; training mode is separate.

Font: Noto Sans KR, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Font and license are included in assets/.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanskr

Speech: AI-generated English voice created through OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts, voice coral. Bright, high-register adult female delivery. Silence trimmed; +2 semitone pitch lift with duration compensation; tempo matched to the previous voice pack; loudness normalized to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP. 56 delivered WAV files in assets/audio; generation and processing provenance in assets/audio/gpt-provenance.json. Generation uses a paid API; playback is offline and requires no key or remote service. The private API key is not included.

Runtime: Godot Engine 4.7.2, MIT license, https://godotengine.org/license/

Test-only dependency: Stockfish.js 17.1.0, GPLv3, https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js . Not shipped in the playable web runtime; used to generate independent campaign victory traces.
